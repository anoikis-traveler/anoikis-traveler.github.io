Anoikis Traveler · payments

For the payee only (Sava Yesh). Payments are separate from donations: only transfers whose reason starts with Anoikis- are read, every 10 minutes, and credited to the map they name.

1 · Link the wallet

Log in with Sava Yesh and allow reading the wallet. The server keeps this permission to read the journal of payments; nothing else. The site's EVE application must allow esi-wallet.read_character_wallet.v1 (developers.eveonline.com).
