🔒 Lock this site to my map

Only your map will exist on this site: nobody can plug in their own EVE application or database, and nobody else can create a map. Players still open your map if you allow them in Access.

This site uses the live server (Cloudflare): no limit on simultaneous connections. Firebase is no longer used.

One-time move: in Firebase → Realtime Database, open the ⋮ menu → Export JSON, then choose that file here. Maps, owners and access lists are copied; nothing is deleted in Firebase.

  1. Upload site.json to your GitHub repository, next to index.html (Add file → Upload files → Commit).
  2. In Firebase → Realtime Database → Rules: replace everything with the locked rules (button above), then Publish.
  3. Reload the map with Ctrl+F5: Settings will show 🔒.

3. Home and hubs

Your EVE login tokens stay in this browser and are only sent to CCP servers. The Firebase database only receives the map and pilot locations.

Help

  1. Log in with EVE login: the map follows your character and adds every system you jump into.
  2. Click a system to see its class, statics, effect, recent activity, your notes and its signatures.
  3. In the in-game scanner, press Ctrl+A then Ctrl+C, and paste into the system's Signatures box.
  4. Click a connection to set the hole type (e.g. H296), mass, end of life, and use the mass calculator.
  5. Log each pass ("Add a pass") to track remaining mass, shared with the whole corp.
  6. Drag systems with the mouse, use the wheel to zoom, drag the background to pan. 3D view rotates around the selected system.
  7. ENTER / EXIT: one click marks your current system as the way in / the way home, a second click removes it.
  8. Thera and Turnur (EVE-Scout data) stay hidden until your chain connects to them: show them with the 🌀 button, and see jumps and routes in the right panel.
  9. 📦 Assets shows where your characters have assets; after each wormhole jump, a window lists the ones within your chosen range.
  10. Set destination (system panel) sends the route straight to your EVE client.
